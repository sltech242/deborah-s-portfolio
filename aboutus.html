About Deborah Paul

Deborah Paul is the creative owner behind a perfume company built around mood, memory, and personal style. Every collection is designed to feel luxurious without being overwhelming—balanced notes, smooth transitions, and a finish that lingers beautifully.

Whether you love fresh florals, warm amber warmth, or bold evening profiles, Deborah Paul’s fragrances are made to help you express who you are—one spray at a time.
