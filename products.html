What Deborah Paul sells

Each fragrance is designed to create a distinct mood—fresh and bright, floral and romantic, or warm and sensual. Here are brief insights into the styles you can expect.

Fresh • Clean • Radiant

Daylight Bloom

A crisp start with airy freshness, softened by delicate floral heart notes—finished with a smooth, subtle base that feels effortless all day.

Floral • Romantic

Velvet Petals

Elegant rose and blooming petals with a refined, creamy warmth underneath—perfect for evenings and memorable entrances.

Warm • Sensual • Signature

Midnight Silk

Deep, comforting notes that unfold slowly—balanced between sensual sweetness and a polished, long-lasting finish.

Citrus • Bright • Confident

Golden Citrus Charm

Bright top notes that lift the mood quickly, transitioning into a fragrant floral-woody blend that stays close and classy.

Soft • Elegant • Everyday Luxury

Chiffon Whisper

A gentle, wearable scent with smooth floral edges and a calm base—ideal for daily comfort and understated sophistication.

Exotic • Bold • Long-Lasting

Amber Desire

A richer profile featuring warm amber warmth and captivating depth—made to linger and leave a memorable trail.
